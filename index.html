About Our School

Shaping Young Minds for
a Better Tomorrow

Greycious Jey International School is a private co-educational institution dedicated to providing holistic, high-quality education from the nursery level through JSS3. We combine academic rigour with moral values, equipping every student with the knowledge, discipline, and character to succeed in life.

Founded with the vision of raising responsible and intellectually strong future leaders, our school provides a safe, structured, and inspiring learning environment where children flourish academically, socially, and morally.

  • Structured curriculum aligned with Nigeria's educational standards
  • Small class sizes for personalized attention and care
  • Regular parent-teacher communication and progress reports
  • Emphasis on moral education, discipline, and character building
  • Vibrant extracurricular and co-curricular activities
